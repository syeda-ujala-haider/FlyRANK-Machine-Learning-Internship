Can Machine Learning Help Editors Prioritize Article Refreshes?

Author: Syeda Ujala Haider

Organization: FlyRank AI

Date: August 2026

1. Problem Statement

FlyRank editors manage thousands of articles across 50+ clients. Each month, they must decide which articles to refresh (rewrite/update) to maintain search rankings.

Challenge: How to prioritize which articles to refresh without a data-driven system?

2. Research Question

Can machine learning create a ranked queue of high-ROI refresh candidates?

3. Data

Dataset: FlyRank/internship-warehouse

Articles: 50,000

Period: June 2026

Clients: 50

Features: ctr_gap, log_impressions, engagement_rate, time_on_page, ai_pct

4. Methodology

Baseline: Hand-written rule = log(impressions) × ctr_gap

Models: Logistic Regression, Random Forest

Validation: 5-Fold GroupKFold (grouped by client)

Metric: Precision@50

5. Results

Model Fold 1 Fold 2 Fold 3 Fold 4 Fold 5 Average
Baseline 0.220 0.120 0.120 0.220 0.040 0.144
LogReg 0.215 0.125 0.130 0.225 0.042 0.147

6. Key Finding

Machine learning adds +0.003 precision (2%) over baseline on known clients, but fails on new clients (0.040 precision).

7. Conclusion

Recommendation: Deploy baseline heuristic, not ML. Same accuracy, simpler, more interpretable.
